# Helio Logistics — Evaluation Reference

Helio Logistics was founded in 2016 by Maya Rao. The company is headquartered in Bengaluru, India, and maintains offices in Singapore and Berlin.

In 2023, Helio Logistics reported revenue of $18.4 million and had 410 employees. In 2024, revenue increased to $24.7 million and employee count increased to 560. The company does not disclose 2025 revenue in this document.

Helio builds route-optimization software for logistics operators. Its flagship product, HelioRoute, launched in 2021. In 2024 the company introduced a demand-forecasting module called HelioForecast.

In March 2024, Helio opened a research laboratory in Bengaluru. In November 2024, it announced a partnership with Northstar Freight to pilot predictive maintenance tools.

Maya Rao is the founder and chairperson. The document does not identify the current chief executive officer or chief financial officer.

The company expanded into Southeast Asia in 2020. Its Singapore office was opened in 2021.
